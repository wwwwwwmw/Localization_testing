# Manual Scenarios (Non-automated / Hard-to-automate)

## 1. Visual fidelity scenarios
1. Kiem tra tinh tham my tong the khi switch locale en/fr/vi/ar.
2. Kiem tra khoang cach, canh le, va icon tren man hinh nho.
3. Kiem tra fallback font khi browser khong co font uu tien.

## 2. Complex RTL scenarios
1. Kiem tra hanh vi mixed-content (Arabic + Latin + number) trong cung paragraph.
2. Kiem tra menu da cap trong RTL.
3. Kiem tra huong animation/carousel trong RTL.

## 3. Exploratory overflow scenarios
1. Thu input chuoi rat dai (>500 ky tu) vao nhieu field.
2. Thu copy/paste doan van ban da ngon ngu vao form.
3. Kiem tra zoom 125% va 150% tren browser.

## 4. Human-language quality scenarios
1. Danh gia ngu nghia ban dich (naturalness) theo ngu canh.
2. Danh gia tone of voice co dong nhat giua page.
3. Danh gia nham lan tu vung chuyen nganh.

## 5. Accessibility exploratory scenarios
1. Kiem tra trinh doc man hinh doc dung language metadata.
2. Kiem tra focus order trong RTL.
3. Kiem tra text-to-speech voi chu co dau.
